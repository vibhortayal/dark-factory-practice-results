## Finding F1 (must fix) — service timestamps run ahead of the real clock under concurrent load

- Cause: `tickStamp` in `stage-3/src/state.js` issues every stamp as `max(Date.now(), lastMs + 1)`, and `clockMs` is `max(Date.now(), lastMs)`. More than one stamp per millisecond pushes the service clock into the future, and it stays there until real time catches up.
