# Verifier check list — Pocketful stage 4

Derived from `pocketful/spec/stage-4.md` (stage-1, 2 and 3 specifications still in force) before any
stage-4 revision existed and without reading the Implementer's work. Scripts: `deliver4.py` (build,
start, limits; runs the two below), `checks4.py` (API: the stage-1, 2 and 3 lists unchanged, then the
stage-4 checks), `ui.py` (the stage-2 browser list unchanged, at 375 and 1280 px, against stage-4).

Sizes: fixtures of 5-60 users, 40-payment histories, batches of up to 33 items, at most 50 requests in
flight.
